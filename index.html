04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOUR
FIRST ROUND.

A few things you might be wondering before you reach out.

Ask us something else ↗
Do I need previous experience?

Tell us whether you’re new to Muay Thai, returning to training, or already practising. We’ll discuss your goals and starting point before arranging a session.

Where does training take place?

Private training is based in Toronto. We’ll confirm the exact location, coach and time with you before you book.

How much does a session cost?

We’ll confirm the session length and rate when we respond to your request, so you have the details before committing.

What equipment should I bring?

Let us know what equipment you already have. Your coach will confirm what you need for the session before you arrive.

How do I request a session?

Share your goals, experience and preferred times in the form below, then open your email draft and send it to the team. Your request starts a conversation; a session is booked once the details and availability are confirmed.

Is this the same as the online programs?

No. This page focuses on private, in-person training in Toronto. Muay Thai Army’s virtual camp and Elite Connect remote coaching are separate options. You can explore them further down this page.
